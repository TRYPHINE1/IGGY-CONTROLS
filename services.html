Our Service Excellence

At IGGY-CONTROLS TECHNOLOGIES, we pride ourselves on being a leading service provider in East and Central Africa. Our commitment is to deliver high-quality, customized repair, installation, and maintenance services for a wide array of laundry and kitchen equipment.

We understand that your business continuity depends on the efficiency and reliability of your assets, which is why we offer a comprehensive suite of technical solutions designed to keep your facility running at peak performance.

Our Four Pillars of Service

Technical Installation

Precision assembly, utility connection (Gas/Steam/Power), and safety-first commissioning.

Preventive Maintenance

Proactive, scheduled servicing designed to minimize wear and prevent costly machine failures.

Curative Maintenance

Expert diagnosis and swift corrective repairs when systems malfunction or break down.

Spare Parts Supply

Reliable procurement of genuine OEM parts for laundry, kitchen, and industrial systems.

Maintenance Scope Details

Technical breakdown of tasks performed under our standard agreements.

01

Preventive Tasks

Performed on a scheduled basis to prevent breakdowns and ensure performance.

  • Inspection of mechanical, electrical, and electronic components.
  • Lubrication of moving parts to reduce friction and wear.
  • Cleaning and descaling of water-contact systems.
  • Calibration of temperature controls, timers, and pressure systems.
  • Replacement of worn consumables (filters, seals, gaskets).
  • Comprehensive performance testing after maintenance.
02

Curative Tasks

Repairs carried out in response to malfunctions or catastrophic failures.

  • Advanced fault diagnosis using professional diagnostic tools.
  • Major component replacement (Motors, Boards, Heating elements).
  • Software and firmware configuration and updates.
  • Re-commissioning and full safety verification post-repair.
  • Technical root-cause reporting to prevent future failures.
