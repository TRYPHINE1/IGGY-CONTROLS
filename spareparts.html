Iggy Controls

Spare-parts support for commercial laundries and service companies.

Iggy Controls helps customers identify and order the correct spare parts for commercial laundry machines. We support laundries, dry cleaners, hotels, care homes and service companies with practical parts identification.
Our spare-parts catalogue is organized around three main equipment categories: Dryers, Ironers and Washers. Search by part name, machine model, brand or part number.
If you are unsure of the exact part, send us the machine nameplate, serial number and a photo of the damaged component. Our technical team can help identify the correct replacement.
